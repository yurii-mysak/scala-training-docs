## Key Interview Questions by Level

**Beginner**: Walk through what happens when you type a URL in the browser. What are the OSI layers? What is the difference between TCP and UDP?

**Intermediate**: How does DNS resolution work? What is NAT and why is it needed? Explain DHCP lease process.

**Advanced**: Explain the TLS 1.3 handshake. How does a reverse proxy differ from a load balancer? What is mTLS and when would you use it?
