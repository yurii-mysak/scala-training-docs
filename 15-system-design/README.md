## Key Interview Questions by Level

**Beginner**: What are the key principles of good software architecture? What is the difference between monolith and microservices?

**Intermediate**: When would you use gRPC vs REST? Explain CQRS and when it adds value. What is the Saga pattern?

**Advanced**: Design a payment processing system. How do you ensure idempotency in distributed transactions? Walk through an event-sourced architecture for an order management system.
