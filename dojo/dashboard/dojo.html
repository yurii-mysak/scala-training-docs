      How it works

      The daily rhythm

      Two lessons arrive at 09:00 Kyiv, by email and here. Lesson 1 is the next rung of one of your tracks. Lesson 2 is researched that morning from fresh sources. At 20:00 a reminder goes out if a lesson is still open.

      Marking a lesson

      Delivered and still open. You can request more only when nothing is open.
      Done. The next sync files it into the knowledge base.
      Comes back for another look a week later, with a review date.
      Not filed. The ladder moves on.

      Marks made here are copied into the repo at the next sync. The repo is the record and this page is its live view. Undo works until you leave the page.

      Commands in the IT Iaido project chat

      passed both
      Mark today's two lessons passed.
      passed 1
      Mark lesson 1 passed. The number is the one on the lesson card.
      review 2
      Send lesson 2 back for review in a week.
      skip 1
      Skip lesson 1.
      next 2
      Two more lessons now, same as Request 2 more.
      pause until <date>
      No deliveries until that date.
      change weight <track> <n>
      How many of every 16 core lessons come from that track.
      add track <name>
      Start a new ladder.
      resend today
      Send today's email again.
